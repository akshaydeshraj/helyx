## Simplify

- `read_bounded/1` repeated the body of the new `bounded/1` error clause. Fixed: the `File.open` error routes through `bounded/1`.
- The property test hard-coded `51_200` four times and `2000` twice, with the generator values derived by hand. Fixed: `@max_bytes` and `@max_lines` attributes; the generator derives from them. The attributes stay an independent oracle and are not read from `Helyx.Tool`.
- The `alias Helyx.Core.Plugins` added for Credo was a trap: it expands inside `Module.concat(name, Plugins)` and silently renames the plugin registry, guarded only by a comment. Fixed at the root: the alias is reverted and `Credo.Check.Design.AliasUsage` is disabled in `.credo.exs` with the reason inline.
- The Kill tool's `{:ok, "never returned"}` after `Process.exit(self(), :kill)` was dead code that lies. Fixed: `@dialyzer {:nowarn_function, run: 2}` and the one-line body; the brutal kill is the point.
- The property generator mixed the giant-line arm into ~2000-line samples, so single samples reached megabytes and the property ran over ten seconds. Fixed: the giant line appears only in small samples; big samples cross the byte total through medium lines. The file runs in ~3 s.
- Skipped: a shared `plt_core_path` (the core Erlang and Elixir PLTs are already shared outside the projects; the per-project PLTs are ~1.5 MB), and deduplicating the bounds vocabulary across the four process docs (each must stand alone for its reader).
